**CPU (Central Processing Unit)**

The **brain of the computer**. It is responsible for carrying out the instructions of a computer program and handles all calculations.

**RAM (Random Access Memory)**

**Temporary, high-speed storage** that the computer uses to hold data for quick access while it's actively working on tasks.

**Storage (HDD/SSD)**

The **long-term memory** where the operating system, all files, and installed programs are permanently saved for future use.
